Portable bundles

Exchange a snapshot as a .uiscope file.

Export a saved snapshot as a portable bundle and import it later. Bundles can be reopened on another machine running UIScope, subject to the format and size checks enforced by the importer.

Offline replay

Open a saved snapshot in the Inspector to review the tree and details without a live device or window capture.

Bundle validation

The importer validates the bundle. Files from a newer app version, damaged bundles or bundles over the documented 100 MB limit may be rejected.

Retention and storage

Set limits without losing pinned references.

Snapshot retention uses configured count and byte caps. Pinned snapshots are exempt from automatic retention; older unpinned snapshots may be removed when a limit is exceeded.

Saved snapshots live under the local UIScope data directory, documented as %LOCALAPPDATA%\UIScope on Windows. They are not synced to a cloud account.
