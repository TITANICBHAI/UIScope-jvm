Four result categories

Turn two captures into a navigable change set.

The Compare screen groups element-level differences so you can focus on what appeared, disappeared, changed or moved between the selected sources.

＋

Added

Elements present in the newer capture that were not matched in the earlier capture.

−

Removed

Elements present in the earlier capture that have no corresponding match in the newer capture.

↔

Changed

Matched elements whose inspected attributes or other tracked fields differ.

↗

Moved

Elements identified as corresponding but whose position or bounds changed.

How matching works

Structural matching, with fallbacks.

The comparison engine uses multiple matching passes rather than relying on a single absolute path. Its implemented strategy considers structural path, identifier and occurrence, content with parent context, and geometric overlap.

A diff is evidence of differences between two captures, not proof of a regression. Dynamic content, unstable identifiers or different capture contexts can affect matching; review the underlying elements before drawing conclusions.
