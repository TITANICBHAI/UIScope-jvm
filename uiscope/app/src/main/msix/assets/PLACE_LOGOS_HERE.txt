Place your MSIX visual asset PNGs in this directory.

Required files and sizes:
  StoreLogo.png         50×50   px   (Microsoft Store listing icon)
  Square44x44Logo.png   44×44   px   (taskbar + start menu small)
  Square71x71Logo.png   71×71   px   (start menu medium — not required but recommended)
  Square150x150Logo.png 150×150 px   (start menu medium tile — required)
  Square310x310Logo.png 310×310 px   (start menu large tile)
  Wide310x150Logo.png   310×150 px   (start menu wide tile)
  SplashScreen.png      620×300 px   (splash screen during cold start)
  BadgeLogo.png         24×24   px   (lock-screen badge — monochrome white)

All PNGs should use a transparent or brand-colored background.
Use target-size variants (e.g. Square44x44Logo.targetsize-16.png) for
crisp rendering at every DPI — see DISTRIBUTION.md §2 for details.

Tools to generate these from a single SVG:
  - https://www.pngtoico.com/
  - Microsoft's Asset Generator in Visual Studio
  - https://github.com/faizanu94/msix-assets-generator
